WHAT IT DOES

Everything
a night needs

Offlyne is small on purpose. Every feature exists to get two people from the same room to the same table

Live only when you are

Offlyne has no endless feed. You appear when you go live at a place you're actually at, and you disappear when you stop. Nothing lingers

Three intentions, one profile

Friends, Dating and Network sit side by side. Pick any combination, change them mid-session, and see at a glance which ones you and someone else share

Venue-aware, not address-aware

The app knows you're at the bar, not where you live. Sort by who's in the room with you or who's a few streets away

A session, not a subscription to someone

Invitations live and die with your session. It keeps things light — and it means no one accumulates a backlog of people who never said hello

Invisible without leaving

Stay live to see who's around you, or go invisible mid-session. That pauses the session and turns your location off — nobody sees you, and you don't see them. The countdown keeps running

Confirm you met, keep the connection

When you both confirm you met in person, the connection outlasts the session. Everything else clears out on its own

Chat built for the next ten minutes to 3 hours

Threads open the moment a request is accepted, with a timer that nudges you to find each other rather than text for a week

Verified humans, reviewed reports

Photo verification is required for your safety before you can go live. Blocks are permanent and invisible to the other person, and reports get read by a person

Built for the density of real places

Restaurants, bars, cafés, conferences, coworking floors. The people you see are the people you could walk up to in the next five minutes

YOUR CALL

Your outing
Your session

Set your intentions, or change them during a session, pause visibility when you want, and end the session on your terms. Invitations belong to that session. But a connection lasts beyond it when you both confirm you met in person. This is Offlyne IRL
